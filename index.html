HOW IT WORKS

Setting Up Your Roster

Add your players and assign each one a role:

  • GRD Guard — your ball-handlers and perimeter players. The planner always tries to keep at least one guard on the court.
  • FWD Forward — versatile players who can play inside or outside. Forwards are used as flexible fill-in spots between guards and bigs.
  • BIG Big — your bigs and post players. The planner tries to keep 2 bigs on the court at all times, with a minimum of 1.

Availability

Mark players ✓ IN or ✗ OUT on game day. Only available players are included in the rotation. You need at least 5.

Game Settings

Tap the quarters × duration chip at the top to set the number of quarters (1–6) and quarter length (5–20 minutes) for your competition.

Interchanges Per Quarter

Choose how many substitution windows to use each quarter:

  • 1 interchange — one swap at the midpoint of each quarter. Fewer stoppages, simpler to manage.
  • 2 interchanges — swaps at the ⅓ and ⅔ marks. More flexibility, useful for larger rosters.

The Rotation Logic

The planner divides each quarter into segments and assigns lineups to give every available player as close to equal minutes as possible. For each lineup slot it:

  • Picks players with the most remaining minutes first
  • Locks in at least 1 (ideally 2) bigs
  • Ensures at least 1 guard is on the court
  • Fills the remaining spots with whoever needs time most — usually forwards

The lineup quality indicator (e.g. 2B 1G) next to each segment shows how many bigs and guards are on court. Green means the target is met, orange means 1 big (minimum acceptable), red means the target wasn't possible given available players.

Courtside Cheat Sheet

A compact summary at the bottom of the generated plan — perfect for reading at a glance during the game.

Fine-Tuning Rotations

After generating a plan, a ✎ Fine-tune button appears below the output. Use this to manually adjust the plan when the algorithm can't account for real-world factors — like a player carrying an injury, or someone who needs reduced minutes.

To make changes:

  • Tap ✎ Fine-tune to enter edit mode — player rows will highlight when hovered or tapped
  • Tap any player name in the rotation plan to swap them with a bench player
  • The swap picker shows only players currently on the bench for that segment, along with their current total minutes
  • Manually changed slots are highlighted in amber with a ✎ icon so you can see what's been adjusted
  • The minutes-per-player summary updates live after every change
  • Tap Done to exit edit mode when you're happy

Use ↺ Reset changes at any time to restore the original generated plan. Your fine-tune edits are saved automatically and will survive a page refresh — but clicking Generate again will wipe them and start fresh.

Installing as an App

This planner can be installed on your phone so it works offline and opens like a native app — no app store required.

🍎  iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Open the app in Safari (must be Safari, not Chrome)
  2. Tap the Share button ⎙ at the bottom of the screen
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add — the app icon will appear on your home screen
🤖  Android (Chrome)
  1. Open the app in Chrome
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top-right corner
  3. Tap Add to Home screen
  4. Tap Add — the app icon will appear on your home screen

Chrome may also show an automatic install prompt at the bottom of the screen on your first visit.

Once installed, the app works fully offline — no internet connection needed on game day.
